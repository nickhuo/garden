# Chelsea Finn

Assistant Professor at Stanford, senior author of **[[DPO]]** ([[2023-05-29 - Rafailov et al - Direct Preference Optimization]]). Best known outside alignment for **MAML** (model-agnostic meta-learning) and robot learning. The DPO group (with [[Rafael Rafailov]], Eric Mitchell, Archit Sharma, Stefano Ermon, Christopher D. Manning) reframed RLHF as direct preference classification.
