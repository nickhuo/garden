# Rafael Rafailov

Stanford researcher, lead author of **[[DPO]]** ([[2023-05-29 - Rafailov et al - Direct Preference Optimization]], NeurIPS 2023), which reduced RLHF preference alignment to a single closed-form classification loss and became a default method for open-weight preference tuning. Works on RL, alignment, and preference-based fine-tuning, advised in the [[Chelsea Finn]] orbit at Stanford.
